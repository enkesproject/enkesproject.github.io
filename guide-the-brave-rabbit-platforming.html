The Brave Rabbit: Platforming Tips for All 10 Levels

By Agus Nugraha · ENKES Project

Meet the Brave Rabbit

The Brave Rabbit is a platformer in which you guide a rabbit through 10 levels, jumping across obstacles and collecting coins along the way. It is simple to pick up, but good timing makes a big difference.

Master the Jump First

Every platformer is built around the jump. Spend your first level getting a feel for how high and how far the rabbit goes. Try short taps and long presses to see the difference, and learn where the jump peaks.

Do Not Rush

It is tempting to hold the move button down the whole time. Slow, controlled movement lets you see what is coming and react in time. Speed is something you add after you know the level.

Collecting Coins Safely

Coins are often placed to tempt you into a risky jump. Before going for one, check what is below it and where you will land. A missed coin costs nothing, while a fall costs progress.

Learning From Each Attempt

Treat every failed attempt as a free look at the level. Notice where you fell and what you could have done differently. By your third try, most sections will feel much easier.

Playing on Mobile

The game runs in any mobile browser. Hold your phone in landscape if the controls feel cramped, and make sure your thumbs are not covering the screen. A stable connection and a closed set of background apps also help keep the game smooth.
