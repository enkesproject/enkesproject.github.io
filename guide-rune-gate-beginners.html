Rune Gate Beginner's Guide: Puzzle First, Battle Next

By Agus Nugraha · ENKES Project

What Is Rune Gate?

Rune Gate is a free HTML5 game that combines two styles of play in one level. First you solve a puzzle. Once the puzzle is finished, the game continues into an automatic battle, so you get to watch the result of your effort unfold on screen. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers, with nothing to install.

Phase One: The Puzzle

The puzzle phase is where you are in control. Take a moment at the start of each level to look at the whole board before you move. Most mistakes in puzzle games come from acting on the first idea instead of the best one. Even five extra seconds of planning can save you a restart.

Phase Two: The Automatic Battle

After the puzzle, the battle plays out by itself. That means your decisions are made before the fight begins, not during it. Treat the puzzle as your preparation: what you do there is the part you can influence, so give it your full attention.

Tips for New Players

Play your first few levels slowly. Learning how the two phases connect is more valuable than finishing quickly.

If a level does not go well, replay it. Each attempt teaches you something about how the puzzle and the battle relate to each other.

Short sessions work best. Ten or fifteen focused minutes are better than an hour of tired play.

If you enjoy the puzzle side of the game, you may also like our block-clearing games, which reward the same kind of careful planning.

Final Thoughts

Rune Gate is built for players who like a bit of thinking followed by a bit of spectacle. Give it a few levels and you will quickly find your own rhythm.
