Getting Started

All our games run directly in your browser — no downloads, no installs. Just open any game page and click Play to begin. Because the games are built with HTML5, they work on both desktop and mobile browsers, though we recommend playing in landscape mode on phones for the best experience.

Puzzle King

Puzzle King is our take on classic block-stacking puzzles. Blocks fall from the top of the screen, and your job is to arrange them into complete rows before the stack reaches the top.

  • Move: Use the left and right arrow keys (or swipe on mobile) to shift a falling block.
  • Rotate: Press the up arrow (or tap the rotate button) to turn a block into a new orientation.
  • Drop faster: Hold the down arrow to speed up the fall.
Tip: Keep your stack flat and avoid leaving gaps — tall, uneven towers make it much harder to clear lines later.

Block Puzzle Extreme

This is Puzzle King's bigger sibling, with 50 levels and a combo system. Clearing multiple lines in quick succession triggers a chain-reaction explosion effect that clears even more blocks around it.

  • Goal: Clear as many lines as possible before the level's block limit runs out.
  • Combo tip: Set up two or three rows to clear simultaneously to trigger the bonus explosion effect.
Tip: Save a few open columns on one side of the board as a buffer for awkward-shaped blocks.

Rune Gate

Rune Gate combines puzzle-solving with automatic battles. You solve a puzzle first, and your performance directly determines how strong your character is in the fight that follows.

  • Puzzle phase: Match or solve the puzzle pattern as accurately and quickly as you can — your score carries over.
  • Battle phase: Your character automatically fights based on the stats earned from the puzzle.
Tip: Higher puzzle scores unlock stronger attacks, so accuracy matters more than speed.

The Brave Rabbit

A classic platformer where you guide a rabbit through 10 levels, jumping across platforms and collecting coins.

  • Move: Left/right arrow keys or on-screen buttons to walk.
  • Jump: Spacebar or the jump button — timing is key to clearing gaps.
Tip: Most levels let you restart instantly if you fall, so don't be afraid to try risky jumps.

General Tips

  • Play on a stable connection to avoid lag on first load.
  • Use fullscreen mode when available, especially on mobile.
  • Replaying earlier levels helps build the pattern recognition needed for harder ones.
