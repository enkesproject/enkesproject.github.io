Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ENKES Project and our games.

Are the games really free?

Yes. Every game on ENKES Project is free to play in your browser. We may show advertisements on the site to help cover hosting and development time.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. All games run directly in your web browser. Some games, such as Puzzle King, can also be added to your home screen so they open like an app. See our guide on installing web games for the steps.

Which devices and browsers can I use?

Our games are designed for phones, tablets and computers. Any modern, up-to-date browser such as Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari should work. If a game runs poorly, try closing other tabs and apps or updating your browser.

A game is slow or freezes. What can I do?

Close other tabs and background apps, reload the page, and make sure your browser is up to date. Our article on fixing slow browser games has more tips. If the problem continues, please contact us with your phone model and browser name.

How do I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Send us a message through the Contact page or email aguspa052@gmail.com. Please tell us which game it is, what happened, and what device you were using. We read every message.

Why do I see ads on the site?

Ads help us keep the games free. We do not ask you to click on ads, and we do not want you to click them by accident. Please read our Privacy Policy to learn how ads and cookies are handled.

Do you collect my personal information?

We do not ask you to create an account to play. For details about cookies, advertising and any data collected by third-party services, please read our Privacy Policy.

Can I use your games on my own website?

Please do not copy or re-host our games without permission. If you would like to feature a game or work with us, contact us first and we will be happy to talk.

Who makes these games?

ENKES Project is a solo indie project by Agus Nugraha. You can read more on the About page.
