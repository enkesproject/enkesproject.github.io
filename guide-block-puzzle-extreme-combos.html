Block Puzzle Extreme: 7 Tips for Bigger Combos

By Agus Nugraha · ENKES Project

Why Combos Matter

Block Puzzle Extreme has 50 levels, and the most satisfying moments come from combos, when several blocks break in a row and the screen lights up with explosion effects. Combos are not just for show. Clearing more at once keeps the board open and gives you room to breathe on harder levels.

Seven Tips

1. Look at all the available pieces before placing any of them. The order you place them in often matters more than where.

2. Keep one side of the board as clean as possible. A tidy side gives you a safe place for awkward shapes.

3. Do not rush to clear a single line. Waiting one move to set up a double clear is usually worth it.

4. Avoid leaving single-cell holes. They are very hard to fill and quietly ruin your layout.

5. Build up one row or column almost to completion, then finish it with the piece that clears several lines together.

6. Keep a mental note of which shapes are hardest for you and leave space for them.

7. When the board gets crowded, switch to survival mode: clear whatever you can and rebuild your structure afterwards.

Staying Calm on Later Levels

Higher levels put more pressure on you, and pressure leads to quick, careless moves. If you notice yourself placing pieces without thinking, pause for a moment. There is no timer on your decisions, only on how long you can keep the board healthy.

Keep Practicing

Combos come with pattern recognition. After a few sessions you will start to see them before you place a piece. If you want a calmer way to practice the same skills, try Puzzle King, which has a more classic feel.
