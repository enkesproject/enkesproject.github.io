About this game

Block Puzzle Extreme turns up the pace: 50 levels of block-stacking that get faster and denser the further you go, with a combo system that rewards clearing blocks back-to-back with a satisfying chain of explosions. It's built for players who already know their way around a puzzle board and want a tighter, faster challenge with a real sense of escalation from level 1 to level 50.

Screenshots

How to play

  • Desktop: arrow keys to move and rotate, down arrow to drop faster.
  • Mobile: swipe left / right to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop.
  • Chain clears back-to-back before the combo timer runs out to trigger bigger explosions.
