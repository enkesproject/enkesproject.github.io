Mob Rush: Getting Started With the Action

By Agus Nugraha · ENKES Project

What Is Mob Rush?

Mob Rush is a fast-paced action game that you can play directly in your browser. It is built with HTML5 and designed to run on both phones and computers, so you can jump in whenever you have a few minutes.

Start With Awareness

In any fast action game, the biggest mistake is tunnel vision. Keep track of the whole screen rather than only the thing you are focused on. Noticing threats early gives you time to react.

Stay Moving

Standing still is rarely safe in an action game. Keep moving with purpose, and choose your path before the screen fills up instead of after.

Learn Through Short Runs

Do not worry about doing well in your first few attempts. Short runs teach you how the game behaves, and every run makes the next one easier.

Take Breaks

Fast games are tiring for your eyes and hands. If you notice your reactions slowing down, take a short break. You will usually come back playing better than before.

More to Explore

If you want something calmer between runs, our puzzle games are a nice change of pace, and our platformer is a good next step for building timing skills.
