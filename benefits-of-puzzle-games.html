Why Puzzle Games Are Worth Your Break Time

By Agus Nugraha · ENKES Project

Small Challenges, Real Satisfaction

Puzzle games give you a clear problem and a clear way to solve it. That small loop of thinking, trying and succeeding is a big part of why so many people enjoy them.

Planning and Pattern Recognition

Games like block puzzles ask you to look at shapes, imagine where they fit, and think a few moves ahead. Practicing this kind of thinking in a low-pressure setting can be a fun mental exercise, though games should be seen as entertainment rather than a substitute for any kind of training or treatment.

A Break That Fits Your Day

A puzzle level can take only a few minutes. That makes it easy to fit between tasks, on a commute, or while waiting. Many players find a short puzzle break a pleasant way to reset before returning to work.

Tips for Healthy Play

Set a rough time limit so a short break does not become a long one.

Rest your eyes every so often by looking at something far away.

Stay hydrated and keep good posture, especially when playing on a phone.

Stop when you feel frustrated. Puzzles are more fun with a clear head.

Where to Start

If you are new to puzzle games, Puzzle King is a gentle introduction. When you want more of a challenge, Block Puzzle Extreme adds 50 levels and combo effects, and Rune Gate combines puzzle solving with an automatic battle.
