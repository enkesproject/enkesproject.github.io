Enkes Project

Hi! I'm an indie developer who loves building simple games and apps. Every project here is made entirely by myself — from concept and design to coding — mostly built with HTML5 so they can be played directly in your browser without any installation.

My Games

Rune Gate

A unique puzzle game where your strategy matters twice. Solve each puzzle level first, then watch your score translate into strength for an automatic battle — the better you solve, the stronger you fight. Can you master both the puzzle and the battlefield?

Kelinci Pemberani

A casual platformer where you control a rabbit that must jump from platform to platform while collecting coins. Featuring 10 levels of increasing difficulty, perfect for a relaxed play session anytime.

Puzzle King

A classic block-stacking puzzle game, reimagined with a fresh look and new features. Test your speed and strategy as you arrange blocks before the screen fills up. Playable directly in your browser or as a downloadable app.

Block Puzzle Extreme

A fast-paced block puzzle game with 50 challenging levels and explosive combo chains every time you clear a block! Stack, clear, and chain your way through increasingly difficult levels for the highest score.

How to Play

All games on this site are free to play directly in your browser, on both mobile and desktop. Just click the link to the game you want to play — it will open instantly, no installation required.
