About this game

Puzzle King takes the block-stacking puzzle format you already know and gives it a fresh coat of paint and a few new ideas. Blocks fall in a steady rhythm and it's up to you to rotate and slot them into place before the stack reaches the top. It rewards both speed and forward planning: clearing multiple lines at once scores far more than clearing them one at a time, so the best runs are the ones where you're already thinking two pieces ahead.

Screenshots

How to play

  • Desktop: arrow keys to move and rotate, down arrow to drop faster.
  • Mobile: swipe left / right to move, tap to rotate, swipe down to drop.
  • Clear full lines to score — clearing several lines at once scores the most.
