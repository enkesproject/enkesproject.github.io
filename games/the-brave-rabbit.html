About this game

The Brave Rabbit is a casual platformer built for a relaxed play session. Guide a rabbit across floating platforms, timing each jump to avoid gaps and collect coins along the way. The 10 levels ease you in gently and get progressively trickier, testing your timing rather than your reflexes under pressure. No lives, no pressure — just hop, collect, and see how far your run takes you.

Screenshots

How to play

  • Desktop: use the arrow keys or WASD to move, and space to jump.
  • Mobile: use the on-screen left / right and jump buttons.
  • Collect coins along the way — some are tucked in spots that need a well-timed jump.
