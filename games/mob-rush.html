About the game

MOB RUSH is a thrilling crowd-runner battle game where one small squad becomes an unstoppable army across 20 action-packed levels.

Your goal: run down the road, grow your blue army as huge as possible, crush every enemy horde in your path, and defeat the mighty Demon Boss waiting at the end of each level.

How to play

  • Steer: drag your finger (or use the arrow keys) to move your troops left and right.
  • Choose your gates: green +N and golden xN gates grow your army, while red -N and ÷N gates shrink it. Choose wisely!
  • Fight: your soldiers fire automatically at the red enemy crowds. Enemies shoot back, chase you down and fight one-on-one, and bigger crowds win bigger battles.
  • Power up: grab rocket and shield power-ups for explosive, crowd-wiping strikes.
  • Win: defeat the Demon Boss at the end of the level and earn up to three stars.

Features

  • 20 themed levels: every level takes place in a unique arena, from Green Meadow and Snow Tundra to Lava Land and Void Zone.
  • Epic Demon Boss fights: each level ends with a horned demon boss with glowing red eyes, fiery roar attacks and bullet storms.
  • Dangerous obstacles: rotating saw blades, chain-exploding TNT, concrete walls and meteor showers.
  • Tougher enemies: elite shielded enemies and mini-bosses.
  • Rescue missions: free cages full of troops waiting to join your army.
  • Star rating: earn up to three stars on every level.

Info

  • Free to play right in your browser, no download needed.
  • Works on phones and computers.
  • Can be added to your phone's home screen like an app.
