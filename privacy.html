Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains what happens with your data when you visit enkesproject.github.io and play the games hosted here.

Data we collect directly

Enkes Project does not run user accounts, does not ask you to sign up, and does not directly collect personal information such as your name or email address just for browsing the site or playing a game. If you choose to email us, we receive whatever information you include in that message.

Cookies and third-party advertising

This site displays advertising from third-party ad networks, including Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites across the web. We do not control these cookies directly — they are set by the ad networks themselves under their own privacy policies.

You can review how Google uses this data at Google's Partner Sites policy, and opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Embedded game content

Some games on this site are embedded from itch.io, a third-party game hosting platform. When a game loads, your browser makes a request to itch.io's servers, which is subject to itch.io's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

This site is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site changes. The "last updated" note above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address on our contact page.
